2. Description of the Service

Post Automator is a social video publishing console built for digital creators, agencies, and businesses. The Service allows users to:

  • Select, preview, and organize digital video content and companion artwork locally on their computers.

  • Publish and schedule videos directly to connected platforms either locally from their machine or via automated cloud-edge triggers (Cloudflare Workers).
