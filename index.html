● TikTok API Usage & Developer Compliance

Post Automator strictly adheres to the TikTok Developer Terms of Service and Partner API Guidelines:

  • video.publish & video.upload: Used exclusively to upload creator-authorized videos from the desktop library directly into their own TikTok feed with user-specified titles and privacy settings.
  • user.info.basic & user.info.profile: Used solely to verify identity, retrieve the creator's username and display avatar in the desktop console workspace.
  • Data Retention: Post Automator does not sell, broker, or aggregate creator data. Revoking access in TikTok immediately disconnects the local authorization session.
