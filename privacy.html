Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Post Automator collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our desktop application and related services.

2. Data Collection and Local Storage

Post Automator operates with a local-first architecture:

  • No Central Cloud Servers: We do not operate external application servers that store your video media, credentials, or personal identity.
  • OAuth Tokens: When you connect a TikTok account, authentication tokens are saved directly to your local operating system keychain (e.g. macOS Keychain).
  • Media Files: All video files, rendered drafts, and project metadata reside on your local machine.

3. Use of TikTok User Data

Post Automator accesses your TikTok profile information (display name, avatar, account handle) solely to display connected account status within the desktop console. We use the TikTok Content Posting API solely to upload and publish videos explicitly selected by you.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or media content. Data is transmitted directly between your local desktop application and TikTok's official API servers using secure HTTPS encryption.

5. User Rights & Disconnection

You can revoke Post Automator's access to your TikTok account at any time through your TikTok account settings or by clicking "Disconnect" in the Connected Accounts section of Post Automator.
